01

Made or Pulled

Returns, Easily

Unworn pieces can be returned within 14 days. Custom and engraved orders are final sale, we'll flag those at checkout.

Materials

Solid metals. Real stones. No plating to wear off.

Everything is cast in 14k gold or sterling silver, set with stones we source from small suppliers we've worked with for years. Nothing in the shop is gold filled or vermeil. It costs more to make it this way, and it lasts longer for it.

Don't see your size or stone?

Most pieces can be resized or set with a different stone on request. Write to us with what you have in mind, and we'll let you know what's possible and what it costs before you order anything.
